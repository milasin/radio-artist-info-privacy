AA Radio Artist Info — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

AA Radio Artist Info ("the app") is developed and maintained by Darko Milasinovic (package name com.darko.radioartist). This document describes what data the app handles and where it goes. The app has no backend service of its own; the developer operates no servers and receives no data from the app.

Summary

  • No accounts. The app never asks you to sign in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no telemetry. The developer collects nothing about you or your usage.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer. Stations, favorites, blocked artists and settings stay on your device.
  • The app does contact third-party services to play radio streams and to identify the song that is playing. They are listed below, together with exactly what each one receives.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private app storage only:

  • Your station list (name, stream URL, country, tags, homepage URL, favicon URL) and the order you arranged it in.
  • Which stations are favorites.
  • Your blocked-artists list.
  • Your settings, including the optional AudD API token and AcoustID API key you may enter yourself.
  • The last station you played.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. If you use Android's backup or device-to-device transfer, your stations, favorites and blocklist are included; the settings file that holds your API keys is deliberately excluded from backups.

While identifying a song, the app writes a short audio sample of the stream to its temporary cache and deletes it as soon as the lookup finishes.

Third-party services the app contacts

The app sends no personal identifiers, device identifiers, account information or location to any of these services. As with any internet request, the service you contact can see your IP address. Every request carries the User-Agent string AARadioArtistInfo/1.0 so the services can identify the app.

Song identification through AudD or AcoustID is off by default and only runs if you obtain and enter your own API credentials in Settings. Those credentials are stored on your device and sent only to the service they belong to.

Permissions the app declares

  • INTERNET — to play streams and contact the services above.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to check network connectivity.
  • WAKE_LOCK — to keep audio playing while the screen is off.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK — required by Android for background media playback with a playback notification.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13 and later) — to show the playback notification. The app asks for this once on first launch; you can decline and playback still works.

The app does not request location, microphone, camera, contacts, calendar, phone or storage permissions. Playing local audio files uses the system file picker, which needs no permission, and the files are not uploaded anywhere.

Android Auto

When the phone is projecting to a car, the car display receives only the now-playing station and track information and album art through Android's standard media-session APIs. Nothing additional is transmitted.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone. Internet radio content is provided by the stations and is unrated.
